03 · The method

The echo method.

The same winding road whether you're a family bakery or a fifty-person firm — only the scale changes.

Phase 01 · Weeks 1–2

Listen

We audit your terrain: analytics deep-dives, customer interviews, competitor mapping. Before a single dollar moves, we know what your audience already murmurs about you.

Phase 02 · Weeks 2–4

Shape

We find the phrase only you can say, build the creative around it, and choose the channels where it will land softest and travel farthest.

Phase 03 · Week 4+

Release

Campaigns go live in small, measured waves. We watch which whispers travel — and feed the ones that do.

Phase 04 · Ongoing

Echo

Weekly notes, monthly pruning, budgets reallocated toward whatever echoes loudest. Small gains compound into the loud part.

…and the echo compounds.
